Information We Collect

NJR10 Live is a football live score application that provides football match scores, fixtures, team information, league standings, and related football content.

Personal Information

NJR10 Live does not require users to create an account or log in to use the application. We do not directly collect personal information such as:

  • Name

  • Phone number
  • Password
  • Home address

We do not require users to provide personal information to access the main features of the application.

Automatically Collected Information

Our third-party service providers, including Google AdMob, may automatically collect certain information when you use the application. This information may include:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers

The information collected depends on the services and SDKs integrated into the application and their configuration.
